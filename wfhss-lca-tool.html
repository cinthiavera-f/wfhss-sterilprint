WFHSS SterilPrint

Every choice shapes tomorrow.
Every action protects life.

Every reprocessing cycle consumes energy and water and emits CO₂. This tool helps CSSD managers measure that impact — and identify what they can change today, at zero cost.

⚠ Pilot v1.2 — Belgrade 2026. Results for decision support and professional learning only. ISO 14044 §4.2.3.3.
SterilPrint
🎯
Purpose
Evidence-based environmental decisions for CSSD managers worldwide. Not for regulatory compliance — for operational awareness and professional learning.
⚗️
Methodology
Carbon Footprint Assessment (ISO 14067:2018) + ISO 14044:2006. Gate-to-gate. Functional unit: one complete reprocessing cycle (WD + packaging + autoclave).
📊
Two output units
① kg CO₂e per CYCLE — comparable across all WFHSS countries. ② kg CO₂e per SET — internal use only. France: CO₂e per Unité d'Oeuvre (SF2S barème).
🌐
5 global clusters
57 WFHSS member countries in 5 clusters by electricity emission intensity. Physical inventory (kWh, L) always directly comparable across all clusters.
Why CO₂e differs between countries — and why that is NOT inefficiency
The electricity emission factor varies up to 47× between WFHSS member countries (Norway 0.017 → Indonesia 0.760 kg CO₂e/kWh). The same autoclave consuming 15 kWh produces very different CO₂e depending on the national grid. Physical inventory (kWh, L) is always directly comparable.

Storage — Expiry Waste ST1
Expired sets = 2× upstream CO₂e · FIFO = zero-cost · ISO 14044 §4.3.4
Sterile set expiry waste ST1—
When a set expires unused: full upstream CO₂e (wash + package + sterilize) is wasted AND the set must be reprocessed again = 2× impact. FIFO rotation + 30-day alerts eliminates this at zero cost.
